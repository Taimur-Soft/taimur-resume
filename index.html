Everything You Need

Powerful features to help you land your next job

Live Preview, Zero Lag

Every keystroke updates the resume beside the form. The preview is exactly what your PDF will look like.

15 Europass-Standard Templates

One EU-recognised structure in three layouts (sidebar, banner, timeline) and 15 styles. Switch instantly without retyping a word, and recolour any template with your own accent.

ATS Check + AI Job Match

Get an instant keyword score, then let Gemini read the job like a recruiter. It matches skills by meaning (JS = JavaScript), flags missing requirements, and rewrites weak bullet points without inventing experience.

Grammar & Tone Coach

LanguageTool catches spelling and grammar mistakes. Gemini scores your tone, spots passive voice, and suggests stronger action verbs.

One-Click GitHub Import

Type your username to pull in your name, bio, location, and up to five top repositories as ready-made project entries.

Link + QR Sharing

Your resume is compressed into the link itself, not stored in our database. Share a short URL, or a QR code for print and mobile.

Your Sections, Your Order

Drag sections into the order that tells your story best. Add custom ones for Certifications, Languages, Volunteering, or Publications.

Cloud Sync + Portable Backup

Sign in to auto-sync multiple resumes, then rename, duplicate, or switch between them. Export to JSON anytime, so your data is never locked in.

Private by Design

Nothing is saved in demo mode. Your Gemini key stays in your browser and goes only to Google. Every field is sanitised against script injection.
